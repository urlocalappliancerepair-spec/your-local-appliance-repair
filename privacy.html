How this first website version handles your information.

Service inquiries

When you submit a request, your contact details and service information are sent securely to FormSubmit, our form delivery provider, for processing and email delivery to Your Local Appliance Repair Inc. We use this information to respond, confirm service details, and arrange a visit. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions in its archive for 30 days. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy. Please call us if you prefer not to use this provider.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking are not currently connected in this preview. When optional analytics are configured, the site will ask for your preference before loading them. Cookie preferences are stored in your browser. A session preference remembers when the service popup has been shown, so it does not interrupt each page you visit. The form does not send names, phone numbers, addresses, brands, or issue descriptions to analytics.

Your choices

You can contact us by phone instead of using the form. Do not include payment-card details or other sensitive information in your inquiry. You can change optional analytics preferences using the footer link.

Contact

For questions about information you have shared, email urlocalappliancerepair@gmail.com or call 437-875-2558.
