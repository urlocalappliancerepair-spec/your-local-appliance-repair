GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you book.

A little clarity goes a long way.

How much is the service call?+

The service-call fee varies by your service area. We confirm the amount before scheduling. If you proceed with the repair, the service-call fee is waived.

Do you offer a repair warranty?+

Yes. We provide a 90-day warranty on parts and labour supplied by Your Local Appliance Repair Inc. Contact us if you experience a problem relating to that work.

Can you come out the same day?+

Same-day appointments may be available, depending on your location and technician availability. Contact us to check. Parts availability can affect when a repair is completed.

Can I get a repair quote over the phone?+

The repair cost depends on the cause of the fault and any parts required. We diagnose the appliance on site and explain the recommended repair and price before you approve the work.

Do you sell appliance parts?+

We provide appliance repair and installation services. We do not operate a parts retail service.

Is a service request a confirmed appointment?+

No. A service request is an inquiry. Our team contacts you to confirm your location, service-call fee, and an available appointment time.
